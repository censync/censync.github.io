Humanized Hash (hh)

v1.1.0 · C++ · Kotlin/Java · TypeScript · Go · Python

An address, a public key or a hash as a small deterministic picture that a person can compare at a glance. Made to catch address poisoning and clipboard substitution. The same pictures, byte for byte, in five languages, with no dependencies and colours chosen for colour vision deficiency.

MHDA

v1.1.0 · Go · C++17

MultiChain Hierarchical Deterministic Address: an RFC 8141 URN that captures everything needed to identify a derived address, from network and derivation path to curve, encoding and wallet context. 14 networks, strict and lenient parsing.
